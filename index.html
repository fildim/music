What it reads

  • Chord sheets — chords above lyrics, or ChordPro [C] brackets
  • MusicXML — .musicxml, .xml, and zipped .mxl
  • MIDI — .mid, .midi

What it can't

  • A PDF or a photo of a score holds a picture of notes, not notes. Export MusicXML from MuseScore, Finale, Dorico or Sibelius and bring that back here.
  • Guitar tablature is left untouched — moving tab means re-fingering it, not renaming it.

How it spells things

  • Transposition moves the letter and the pitch together, so in E→F an A chord becomes B♭ and not A♯.
  • Key signatures, printed accidentals and slash-chord bass notes all follow.
  • Everything runs in your browser. No file you open is uploaded anywhere.
